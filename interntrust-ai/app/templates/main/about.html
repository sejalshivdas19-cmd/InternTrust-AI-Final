Why Students Need Opportunity Verification

Every year, students actively looking for internships and jobs receive offers through WhatsApp, Telegram, LinkedIn, email, and job boards. Many are genuine. Some are not — and the warning signs are not always obvious to someone applying for their first opportunity.

Common Warning Signs
  • Requests for a registration, training, or security deposit fee
  • Recruiters using personal email addresses instead of company domains
  • Unrealistic pay for minimal work, or guaranteed placement claims
  • Urgency, pressure, or threats to act "right now"
  • Vague job descriptions with no verifiable company information
How InternTrust AI Helps
  • Runs a transparent, explainable rule-based risk engine on every submission
  • Optionally uses AI to summarize and explain findings in plain language
  • Never declares an opportunity "fraudulent" — only flags signals for review
  • Provides a practical, step-by-step verification checklist
  • Keeps a private history of every analysis you've run
Responsible Use

InternTrust AI is a decision-support tool, not a verdict engine. It is designed to help students ask better questions and take safer next steps — the final judgment always remains with the user.
